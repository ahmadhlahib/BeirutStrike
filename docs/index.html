Features

Everything in the game

A fast online shooter for your phone, built around the city itself.

Real Beirut streets

Five neighbourhoods built from real map data: building shapes and heights, roads, parks, squares, trees and the sea, with sandstone, plaster and glass facades, shop fronts and street lamps.

A living city

Traffic drives the streets with wheels that turn, and people walk the sidewalks, chat on the phone and run for cover when the shooting starts.

Play solo against bots

No one online? Fight 1 to 8 bots on Easy, Medium or Hard, with some on your team if you like. They hunt you down, take cover and shoot back.

Team voice chat

Talk to your teammates during the match. Your mic stays off until you turn it on, and only your team can hear you.

12 real guns

Pistols, rifles, machine guns and sniper rifles, each with its own 3D model, sound, recoil, magazine and reload.

Money and guns

Earn money for every kill, headshot and win online, and buy the guns you want for good. Sign in with Google and they follow you to any phone.

Arms stores and medkits

Every map has arms stores selling magazines, scopes, grenades and medkits. Heal when you choose: a small medkit gives a heart back, a big one fills them all.

20 military ranks

Earn XP with every kill, headshot and win. Each promotion brings a new badge and a rank-up celebration.

Rooms with friends

Create a room: pick the map, the play area and the match length, add a password or a minimum rank, and invite your friends.

Teams and factions

Fight for one of three factions, or create your own team with its own name, flag and colour. The map shows your teammates, and enemies only as rough red circles.

Make it yours

Choose your character, celebrate wins with a victory dance, put your face on the soldier, talk with speech bubbles and leave photos in the streets.

Army kit

Every fighter wears army camouflage and boots in their team's colours, so you can tell friend from foe at a glance.

Maps

Arsenal

12 real guns

Before each match, carry one pistol, one primary and one sniper rifle, and switch between them in the city. The Beretta M9, AK-47 and SVD are free; the others are bought with money you earn, from $1,500 to $12,000, and kept for good. Ammo runs out: pick up magazines in the streets or buy them at an arms store.

How to play

Controls

Hold your phone sideways. Everything is on screen.

Red button (under the minimap)Shoot. Hold it for automatic guns, and slide your finger on it to aim while you fire
Second red button (above the joystick)Shoot with your left thumb: works like the big one

Timer and minimapOpen the scoreboard, or the full map (your teammates, red circles roughly where enemies are, and green $ for the stores)

Just for fun

Cheat codes

Create a room with Allow cheats (just for fun) turned on, then type a code in the Say box. Other players don't see it, and it only changes the game on your phone. Capitals, extra spaces and a final "!" don't matter.

CodeWhat it does
unlimited ammoYour bullets never run out
unlimited healthHits never cost a heart, and your health goes back to full
find a scopeGives you a scope; tap the scope button to aim further
full healthYour health goes back to full, once
super speedWalk and run twice as fast
rapid fireYour guns fire automatically, twice as fast
cancel cheatsTurns every cheat off

Cheat rooms are marked "Cheats on · not ranked" in the room list. Matches there still have a scoreboard, but they don't count toward the Ranking, XP or military ranks, for anyone in the room. In a normal room, a code just shows "Cheats are off in this room".

FAQ

Questions

Can't find your answer? Email Ahmad.h.lahib@gmail.com.

Is Beirut Strike free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads and nothing to buy with real money. The money in the game is earned by playing.

Do I need an account?

No. Type a player name and play as a guest; your progress stays on your phone. If you sign in with Google, your XP, rank, money and guns follow you to any phone. There's no password of ours.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or newer, with OpenGL ES 2.0 graphics (almost every phone from the last several years). The game is played with the phone held sideways.

How do I play with my friends?

Create a room, choose the map and match length, and add a password. Share the password with your friends; only they can join. You can also set a minimum rank so only experienced players get in.

How do I rank up?

Play online matches in rooms without cheats: every kill gives 50 XP, a headshot kill 25 more, and winning a match 500. Your rank goes from Private (level 1) to Beirut Legend (level 20, at 45,500 XP).

How does voice chat work?

In an online room, tap the mic button to talk to your teammates; tap it again to stop. Only your team hears you, and the other team never does. Your voice goes straight from phone to phone while you talk and is never recorded or stored. The speaker button mutes all your teammates.

Can I play without internet?

Yes: choose Solo and play against bots on your phone, no internet needed. Solo games are practice, so they don't give XP or money. Playing with others needs the internet.

How do I get money and new guns?

Online, every kill gives $300, a headshot $100 more and winning a match $1,000 (not in solo games or rooms with cheats). Spend it on the guns screen: a gun you buy is yours for good. At the arms stores in each map you can also buy magazines, a scope, grenades and medkits during a match.

Is it safe for my data?

The game collects only what it needs to run: your player name, a user ID (and your Google email and name, only if you sign in with Google), game stats and the photos and messages you choose to share. Read the privacy policy. You can delete everything from the menu with Delete my data, or by email.

What if someone is abusive?

Open Players in the game menu, or a photo someone dropped, to report or block them. You can also report a team by long-pressing it. Reports are reviewed, and offending content is removed.
