Delete your data

Beirut Strike by Ahmad EL Lahib

You can delete everything Beirut Strike keeps about you at any time. Beirut Strike has no accounts or passwords: your data is linked to your player name and an anonymous ID on your phone.

Two ways to delete it

In the game (instant)

  1. Open Beirut Strike and enter the city.
  2. Tap your name, top left, to open the menu.
  3. Choose Delete my data and confirm.

Your data is deleted right away, from our servers and from your phone, and you're signed out.

By email (no app needed)

If you uninstalled the game or can't open it, email us with the player name you used. We delete your data within 30 days and reply when it's done.

Email a deletion request

Ahmad.h.lahib@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your face photo
  • Your career: career score, kills, deaths and other totals, XP and military rank, and wins
  • Your match scores and the photos you dropped in the city
  • Your anonymous account (the random user ID)
  • Everything the game stored on your phone (in-game deletion only; uninstalling the game also removes it)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Room data (your position, speech bubbles, teams you created in a room) is already deleted automatically once the room is left empty, usually within minutes.
  • Reports other players sent about you are kept only while we review them, then deleted.
  • Nothing else is kept. We don't keep backups of deleted data.
