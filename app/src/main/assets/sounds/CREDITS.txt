Gun recordings from Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org). Each was cut (to a single shot, or to one movement of a reload), mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, faded and normalised; the game plays some slower or faster to suit other guns. The adapted recordings are shared under the same licence as the originals.

Beeld en Geluid (the Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision), CC BY-SA 3.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0):
- rifle_ak47.wav — "AK-47 (Kalasjnikov), een schot". AK-47 and M4A1.
- lmg_fnmag.wav — "FN MAG, een schot". RPK and M249.
- sniper_fnfal.wav — "FN FAL, een schot". SVD, M24 and AWM, and slowed down for the Barrett M82.
- smg_uzi.wav — "Palm kazerne, Uzi machinegeweer". MP5.
- mag_out.wav, mag_in.wav, rifle_charge.wav — "Magazijn laden en het vergrendelen van een geweer". Magazines going out and in, charging handles, bolt actions and the M249's feed cover.

Mike Koenig, uploaded by Dantedun, CC BY-SA 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0):
- pistol_9mm.wav — "9 mm gunshot". Beretta M9 and Glock 17, and slowed down for the Desert Eagle.
- pistol_slide.wav — "Gun Cocking Slow". Pistol slides.

The M249's belt rattle and the cries are generated by the game (SoundSynth).
